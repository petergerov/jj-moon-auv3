# Space

Last block before the master mix — a short stereo double into a soft acoustic
room. Deliberately not a spring tank and not a hall.

## Double

A Haas / flutter double: short modulated delays (~22 / 31 ms), L/R LFO phases
offset the way jj-midnight offsets slapback — so a single dry copy does not
sound pasted behind the note. Cross-blended a little so it widens rather than
sitting as two mono echoes.

## Room

Early reflections into a single recirculating delay with a low-pass in the
feedback path. Size stretches the taps and the tank; bigger rooms also get
darker (more air absorption). Mix is the wet level.

## Controls

| Control | Range | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Double** | 0–100 % | Level of the stereo flutter double. |
| **Size** | 0–100 % | Room size (taps + tank). Bigger also darker. |
| **Room** | 0–100 % | Wet level of the bloom. |
| **On/Off** | | Bypasses double and room together. |

## What to reach for

- **Close Mic** — Double and Room almost out
- **Moon Room** — Size and Room up, Curve moderate
- **Fallen Leaf** — a little room air around a body-forward curve
