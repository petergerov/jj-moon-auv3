# Marketing notes — jj-moon

Working notes for listing copy and naming. Paste-ready App Store fields live
in [APP_STORE_SUBMISSION.md](APP_STORE_SUBMISSION.md). The public site is
[docs/index.html](docs/index.html).

## Positioning

jj-moon is the **acoustic** sibling of jj-breeze (width / vocal colour) and
jj-midnight (vintage clean electric chain). Same panel family, different job:

> Body, air, and string detail — the shape of a well-mic'd acoustic, in one
> insert.

Lead with **Curve**. Comp, Width and Space are the supporting cast.

## Rules (same family)

- No artist, band, or song titles in the App Store listing.
- Never claim the plug-in reproduces a particular record or mic setup.
- Preset names describe a feel or a place; keep them short.

## Name

**jj-moon** — decided. Bundle IDs, AU subtype `Jjmo`, App Group, and IAP all
use `jjmoon`. Distinct from breeze (`Jjb3`) and midnight (`Jjm1`).

## One-liners

- Subtitle: `Acoustic guitar curve for AUv3`
- Promo: `Acoustic guitar curve, soft comp, stereo width and room — one AUv3 insert.`
